# Tasks and Notifications

---

## Tasks

**Tasks** are the steps left to take in a workspace, and the work Meltano is doing for it. Only the workspace's administrators see them, and every administrator sees the same ones.

Find them in two places:

- **Tasks** in the sidebar: the full list, with each task's status and when it was added.
- **Workspace Tasks** at the top of the page: the latest few, with a count, and **See All**.

| Task | Added | Done |
|---|---|---|
| **Import Data** | With a new workspace. | Once its first ingestion is created. |
| **Invite Your Team** | With a new workspace. | Once anyone invites someone to it, from [Team](/meltano-cloud/team). |
| **Complete the Configuration** | When an ingestion, workflow or warehouse from the workspace's repository couldn't be set up in full, so it was saved as a draft. | Once it is saved with every setting it needs. |
| **In Progress** | While Meltano sets the workspace up, saves changes to it, or prepares an ingestion. | When that work ends. It can't be cleared. |

A to-do goes by itself once it is done. Choose **Open** to go where it is done, or **Clear** to remove it if you don't need it. **Clear All** removes every to-do. Runs and deployments are not listed here: see [Run History](/meltano-cloud/run-history) and [Deployment History](/meltano-cloud/workspace-settings#deployment-history).

---

## Notifications

The **bell** at the top of the page shows your notifications, with a count of the unread ones. They are yours, across all your workspaces: for example the runs of the ingestions and workflows you [watch](/meltano-cloud/run-history#watch-an-ingestion-or-workflow).

- Click a notification to open the ingestion or workflow it is about. That marks it read.
- **Mark All as Read** marks every one read.
- **Clear All** removes them.
