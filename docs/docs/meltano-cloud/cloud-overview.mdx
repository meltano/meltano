## Why teams use Meltano Cloud

Meltano Cloud is the fastest way to run production ELT pipelines without managing infrastructure. Sign up, choose a warehouse and your sources, and Meltano handles the rest: deployments, scheduling, credentials and monitoring are all managed for you.

## Why companies choose Meltano Cloud

- **No infrastructure to manage**: workspaces, deployments and scheduling are handled for you.
- **Git-backed by design**: every workspace is backed by a Git repository, so your pipeline configuration stays version-controlled and auditable.
- **Isolated by team or project**: each workspace has its own ingestions, workflows, warehouses, members and credentials.
- **Credentials handled safely**: secrets are entered in the app, stored encrypted, and never written to the repository.

## Get started

1. **Sign up** at [meltano.com](https://meltano.com) with your work email (**TRY MELTANO**).
2. **Set up your workspace** with the setup wizard: name it, choose a warehouse (Meltano-managed PostgreSQL needs no credentials) and pick your sources. See [Onboarding](/meltano-cloud/onboarding).
3. **Run an ingestion**: open **Ingestion**, where each source is an [ingestion](/meltano-cloud/ingestion). Click **Run**, or let it run on its schedule.
4. **Follow it**: watch the run and its log in [Run History](/meltano-cloud/run-history), and **Watch** the ingestion to get an email if it fails.
5. **Invite your team** from [Team](/meltano-cloud/team).
6. **Go further**: reload history with [Backfills](/meltano-cloud/backfills), run dbt and more with [Workflows](/meltano-cloud/workflows), connect your own [warehouse](/meltano-cloud/warehouses), and let [Meltano AI](/meltano-cloud/diagnose-with-meltano-ai) explain failures.

:::note The Classic UI
**Classic UI**, at the bottom of the sidebar, opens Meltano Cloud's previous interface. It still has Datasets and the Lab's other apps.
:::
