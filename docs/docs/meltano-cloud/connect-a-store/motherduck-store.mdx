# MotherDuck Store

## Create a new database

To create a new database in MotherDuck, follow these steps:

1. Go to https://app.motherduck.com/home and log in to your MotherDuck account if you're not already logged in.
1. Look for the "Attached databases" section in the left sidebar.
1. Click on the + ("Add database") button.
1. Enter a name (e.g. `meltano`) for your database and click "Create database".
1. Copy the name of your database.

## Create an access token

To create an access token for your MotherDuck database, follow these steps:

1. Go to https://app.motherduck.com/settings/tokens and log in to your MotherDuck account if you're not already logged in.
1. Click "Create token".
1. Enter a name and optional expiration date for your token and click "Create".
1. Copy the token and store it securely.

## Configure Meltano

To configure your Meltano Workspace to use your MotherDuck database, follow these steps:

1. Add a new store to your Meltano Workspace using the `motherduck` store type.
1. Use `md:<your-database>` as the connection string, replacing `<your-database>` with the name of your database.
1. Paste in the access token you copied from the MotherDuck settings page.
