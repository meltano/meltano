# Team

Open **Team** in the sidebar to see everyone in the workspace and the invitations waiting to be accepted.

---

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Administrator** | Change the workspace's settings, add its ingestions, workflows, warehouses and plugins, and choose who else administers it. |
| **Member** | See everything in the workspace, but not change its settings or add ingestions, workflows, warehouses or plugins. |

The owners of the account the workspace belongs to can manage every workspace in it, whatever their role in the workspace.

---

## Invite someone

Anyone in the workspace can invite someone.

1. Click **Invite** at the top right. The **Invite Member** drawer opens.
2. Enter their **Email**.
3. Click **Send Invitation**.

They get an email with a link to join the workspace as a **Member**. An administrator can make them an administrator once they have joined.

If the workspace has **Approved domains** (in [Settings → General](/meltano-cloud/workspace-settings#general)), only addresses in those domains can be invited.

---

## Pending invitations

Invitations not yet accepted are listed under **Pending Invitations**. From an invitation's **⋮** menu:

| Action | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **Resend** | They didn't get the email. Inviting the same address again also sends it again. |
| **Revoke** | You want to cancel it. The link stops working. Whoever sent it, or an administrator, can revoke it. |

---

## Manage members

Administrators can use a member's **⋮** menu:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Make Administrator** | Gives the member an administrator's rights. |
| **Revoke Administrator** | Takes them back; they stay a member. |
| **Remove from Workspace** | They lose access to the workspace and any administrator rights in it. They can be invited again. |
